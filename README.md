## 🚀 About Me

Software Quality (QA) professional with more than **17 years of experience in technology**, working with **test strategy, automation, technical leadership, and QA team management**.

I have experience in large-scale and high-complexity projects across sectors such as **Banking, PKI & Digital Certificates, Information Security, Healthcare, Tax Systems, Mobile, POS & TEF, and Gaming**, always focused on **quality, reliability, performance, and test governance**.

I am recognized for acting as a **bridge between business, product, and engineering**, fostering a strong quality culture, technical growth within teams, and continuous process improvement.
